## The short version

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an amount">
    Anything from $5 to $1,000. You pay exactly the card's value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay from your wallet">
    Scan a QR code with any Solana wallet and send SOL or USDC.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spend it">
    Your card number, expiry and security code appear in your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A Havn card is issued in US dollars and works at US online merchants. Check
  [Your card](/cards/types) before buying one so you know what to expect.
</Note>
