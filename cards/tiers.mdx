# Choosing an amount

Any amount from **$5 to $1,000**. Pick what you plan to spend — the card is loaded once and cannot be topped up.

<img src="/images/havn-travel-crypto.webp" alt="Spending a Havn card" className="my-8 rounded-xl max-w-[420px] mx-auto block w-full" />

## A rough guide

| Amount | Good for |
| :--- | :--- |
| **$5 – $50** | A subscription, a one-off purchase, trying Havn for the first time |
| **$50 – $250** | Regular online shopping, a few months of subscriptions |
| **$250 – $1,000** | A single larger purchase |

These are suggestions, not tiers. There is no status to unlock and no difference in how a $10 card and a $900 card behave — same network, same acceptance, same fee of nothing.

## More than $1,000

Buy several cards. There is no limit on how many you can hold, and they appear together in your account.

<Tip>
  For a specific purchase, order the exact total including tax and shipping. A
  card with slightly too little on it will be declined, and the leftover on a
  card with too much cannot be moved.
</Tip>
