# Mobile app

The Havn app is **coming soon** to the App Store and Google Play.

It will do what the website does — buy a card, see your balance, check what you have spent — built for a phone.

<Card title="Get notified" icon="x-twitter" href="https://x.com/HavnFinance">
  We announce releases on X. That is where the launch date will appear first.
</Card>

## Until then

The website works on mobile. Open it in your phone's browser, and when you pay, the QR code screen also offers an **Open in wallet** button that hands the payment straight to a Solana wallet app on the same device.

## What we are not promising yet

We would rather under-promise here than list features that do not exist:

- **Apple Pay and Google Pay** are not supported today. Havn cards are virtual and made for online checkouts, not contactless payments in shops.
- **Physical cards** are not available.

If either changes, it will be announced on X and documented here first.
