# Fees

**Havn charges no fee.** A $50 card costs $50.

<img src="/images/havn-zero-fees.webp" alt="No fees" className="my-8 rounded-xl max-w-[420px] mx-auto block w-full" />

What you do pay, and what we do not control:

| Cost | Who charges it | Typical |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Havn fee | — | **$0** |
| Solana network fee | The network | A fraction of a cent |
| SOL to USDC conversion | Only if you pay in SOL | Market rate plus slippage |

Paying in **USDC avoids the conversion entirely** — the amount you send is the amount loaded onto the card.

## Limits

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Minimum | **$5** per card |
| Maximum | **$1,000** per card |
| Reloadable | No — one load per card |
| Currency | US dollars |

Need more than $1,000? Buy more than one card. There is no limit on how many you can hold.

## What affects the final amount

If you pay in **USDC**, nothing does. You send the amount shown and the card is loaded with it.

If you pay in **SOL**, the quote includes a small buffer because SOL's price moves between the moment the QR code is generated and the moment your transaction is confirmed. Without it, a dip mid-payment would leave the order short and the card could not be issued.

<Tip>
  Order the exact amount you plan to spend. Cards are not reloadable, and a
  leftover balance on a card you have finished with cannot be moved to another.
</Tip>
